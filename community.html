ENGINEERING IS BETTER WHEN SHARED

Making engineering accessible through hands-on learning, student leadership, and community engagement.

Why I share STEM

I believe STEM is most meaningful when it moves beyond textbooks and becomes something people can explore, build, and experience for themselves. Through engineering clubs, educational outreach, and student-led ventures, I seek to make technical learning more accessible while turning ideas into practical solutions that benefit others.

STEMIFY

Turning engineering concepts into educational toys while reinvesting profits in community initiatives.

I lead Stemify, a student-run venture that creates balance-scale toys to make mathematical and physical concepts more tangible. I oversee product design, 3D printing, branding, marketing, and sales, bringing educational ideas from prototype to market.

Through direct customer outreach and product sales, I have helped sell 100+ product sets, generating approximately $960 in revenue. I allocated 100% of profits—approximately $460—to YES community initiatives, supporting scholarships, Tet gifts, and STEM activities for children with limited access to educational resources.

Stemify has shown me how engineering and entrepreneurship can work together: a product can create value not only through what it teaches its users, but also through the opportunities its success helps provide to others.

PHYSIX

Bringing hands-on STEM learning to students across Ho Chi Minh City and Gia Lai.

Across Ho Chi Minh City and Gia Lai, I have helped deliver 13 interactive lessons and experiments in physics, engineering, and environmental science to more than 100 students. AEROLEAGUE: THE PLANE ENGINEERS

FounderJanuary 2026 – Present

Exploring aircraft engineering through model building, aerodynamics, and experimentation.

I founded AeroLeague to introduce students to aircraft engineering through hands-on model-building. I lead a 10-member club that meets weekly to explore aerodynamics, aircraft components, and fundamental engineering principles. I also guide 12 students in building model airplanes and gliders from wood and polystyrene, culminating in a competition where they test and refine their designs to maximize flight distance.

Building Beyond the Classroom
