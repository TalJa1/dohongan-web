RESEARCH 01

Sustainable Wastewater Treatment & Reuse

An Giang, Vietnam

Techno-economic analysis of domestic wastewater treatment and reuse models for apartment-scale systems in An Giang, Vietnam.

I investigated approaches to making wastewater treatment more economically viable and sustainable in An Giang, Vietnam, where wastewater infrastructure remains uneven, particularly in peri-urban areas. My research compared three treatment models—MBBR, MBR, and BASTAF–CW—by examining treatment performance, life-cycle costs, affordability, and opportunities for water reuse.

The study identified BASTAF–CW as the most cost-effective option among the evaluated models, with an estimated treatment cost of approximately $0.14/m³. We also examined a locally sourced greywater treatment module that reduced influent pollutant loads by 30–40%. This research deepened my understanding of how engineering decisions must balance technical performance with economic feasibility and local resource availability.

RESEARCH 02

Robotic Arm Design, Simulation & Control

Application of Computer Science and MATLAB to the Design, Simulation, and Control of a Product-Sorting Robotic Arm

I collaborated on research exploring the application of computer science and robotics to automated product sorting. Using MATLAB and Simscape Multibody, we designed and simulated a three-degree-of-freedom (3-DOF) robotic arm, developing its forward and inverse kinematics, Denavit–Hartenberg parameters, and trajectory-planning methods.

The project integrated a graphical user interface (GUI) for real-time control and visualization. Simulation results were used to evaluate the arm’s movement, trajectory accuracy, and feasibility for automated sorting applications. Through this work, I explored how mathematical modeling, computational simulation, and control systems can come together to solve practical engineering problems.
