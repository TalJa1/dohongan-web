CLEAN WATER EMPOWERMENT PROJECT

PURE MEKONG

Clean Water, Healthier Communities, Sustainable Futures

Bridging engineering innovation and community needs to make clean water more accessible.

FOUNDER

I founded Pure Mekong out of a desire to understand and address the challenges of clean-water access in Vietnam’s Mekong Delta. In a region where drought, saltwater intrusion, and water contamination affect daily life, I began exploring a question that continues to guide my work:

How can scientific knowledge and engineering be translated into solutions that communities can realistically use?

  • 150+residents interviewed
  • 1,050 Lwater delivered
  • 100+user interviews
  1. 01

    Understanding the Problem

    I conducted surveys and interviews with more than 150 residents across two Mekong Delta provinces to learn about local water shortages, contamination, and barriers to accessing clean water. These conversations helped me recognize the importance of understanding community needs before proposing technical solutions. I also organized three educational workshops to promote water conservation and raise awareness of local water challenges.

    Images: RFI · VnEconomy · GiveNow

  2. 02

    Turning Research into Action

    Recognizing that some communities face immediate water shortages, I raised $570 to provide 1,050 liters of drinking water to residents in need. This initiative allowed me to connect long-term research with practical support for communities facing urgent challenges.

    Beyond immediate relief, I led research and development of a low-cost water filtration system designed to improve water quality for household use. Through more than 100 user-feedback interviews and field evaluations, I explored the system’s usability and suitability for local conditions, learning to consider not only technical design but also accessibility and the realities of everyday use.

  3. 03

    Looking Beyond the Technology

    Pure Mekong has shaped my understanding of engineering as more than the process of designing a functional system. A meaningful solution must begin with listening, respond to real circumstances, and remain grounded in the needs of the people it serves. Through this project, I have learned to approach environmental challenges with both scientific curiosity and a stronger sense of social responsibility.

    The project’s clean-water initiatives have also received local media coverage, reflecting the importance of community-centered efforts to improve access to clean water.

Engineering Projects

PROJECT 01

Water Filtration System

  • Engineering
  • IoT
  • Water Technology

I developed a multi-stage water filtration system designed to reduce salinity and iron contamination, exploring an affordable approach to improving household water quality.

The system integrates sediment filtration, activated carbon, ion exchange/reverse osmosis (RO), and an Arduino/ESP32-based monitoring setup with TDS and pH sensors. I tested the system with 60 users and incorporated 55 feedback responses to refine its design and usability.

I later donated the system to Dong Yen 3 Primary School in An Giang, supporting continued field testing and further development of accessible water-treatment technology.

PROJECT 02

IoT Salinity Intrusion Warning System

  • IoT
  • Environmental Engineering

Building on my work in water filtration, I developed an IoT-based early-warning system to monitor salinity intrusion and water-quality conditions in real time. The project focuses on helping agricultural and aquaculture communities identify potential risks and make more informed decisions.

The system integrates an ESP32, DS18B20 temperature sensor, TDS and pH sensors, and a water-level sensor with Blynk Cloud, Wi-Fi/4G connectivity, and mobile/web dashboards. I tested the system with 54 users and used 35 feedback responses to guide further improvements.

I am continuing field testing in agricultural and aquaculture settings, with plans to improve sensor accuracy and expand automated alerts for salinity monitoring.
