I joined Run & Rise with an interest in sports, but the experience gradually showed me that running is about more than speed or endurance. It is also about encouraging one another, working toward shared goals, and creating a community where everyone can challenge themselves and grow.

What I Learned

Organizing Run4Real Season 2 showed me how a shared passion for sports can become a source of positive change. I learned that leadership goes beyond managing logistics; it means bringing people together around a purpose and helping turn collective effort into tangible support for others. For me, the finish line is not the end of a race, but the beginning of what we can accomplish together.
