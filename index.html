WHERE IDEAS FLOW

I’m Hong An Do - Andy

I have always been curious about how things work — and how engineering can turn ideas into solutions. I enjoy exploring STEM, building things with my hands, and asking how science and technology can respond to problems beyond the classroom.

One issue that has particularly caught my attention is access to clean water in the Mekong Delta. Water is everywhere, yet clean and safe water is not always equally accessible. This has inspired me to explore how engineering and STEM can create practical, affordable solutions for communities facing water-related challenges.

For me, engineering is more than building machines. It is about following a question, testing an idea, and letting curiosity flow into something that can make a difference.
