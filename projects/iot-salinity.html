IoT Salinity Intrusion Warning System

  • IoT
  • Environmental Engineering
May 2026 - Present
  • Developed an IoT-based early-warning system to monitor salinity intrusion and water quality in real time, helping communities respond to risks affecting irrigation and aquaculture.
  • Integrated ESP32, DS18B20, TDS, pH, and water-level sensors with Blynk Cloud, Wi-Fi/4G, and mobile/web dashboards; tested by 54 users and refined through 35 feedback responses.
  • Deploying the system for field testing in agricultural and aquaculture settings, with plans to improve sensor accuracy and expand automated alerts for real-time salinity monitoring.
