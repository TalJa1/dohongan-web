Stemify

  • 3D Printing
  • Entrepreneurship

A student-led business project developing and selling STEM educational toys for children.

  • Led the design, 3D printing, branding, marketing, and sales of student-made products, managing the project as a real-world entrepreneurial venture.
  • Sold 100+ 3D-printed product sets, generating approximately $960 in revenue through product sales and direct customer outreach.
  • Allocated 100% of profits (~$460) to support YES community initiatives, including scholarships, Tet gifts, and STEM activities for underserved children.
